## Overview

Army Jay ("the App") is a toolkit for FPV pilots that converts audio for EdgeTX radios and edits Betaflight OSD fonts, developed by Jason Chalky. **We do not collect any personal information, we do not require an account, and nothing you make in the App is sent off your device.**

## What We Collect

**Nothing.**

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier
- Your location, even approximate
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
- Contacts, photos, calendar, or any other data from your device
- Analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage metrics
- The audio files or images you open in the App

The App makes no network requests.

## What We Store (on your device only)

The App keeps the following in its own storage **on your device**, never on a remote server:

- Sounds you have converted (up to the limit shown in My Sounds)
- Your edits to OSD font glyphs
- Your boot splash design, including any image you chose for it

Files you open (audio from Files, images from Photos or Files) are read on your device only when you choose them. Files you save are handed to the iOS share sheet, and go only where you send them.

This data leaves your device only if **you** enable iCloud backup for the App in iOS Settings. Apple handles iCloud backups; we have no access to them.

To delete all stored data, use **Clear all** in My Sounds and **Discard all edits** in the glyph editor, or delete the App from your device.

## Third-Party Services

None. The App contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising.

The **Source** link at the bottom of the App opens the project's GitHub page in Safari. GitHub's privacy policy applies there: [docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children's Privacy

Army Jay is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data Security

Everything the App stores stays in its sandboxed storage on your device, protected by standard iOS file-system encryption when your device is locked. We never transmit, process, or store your data on servers we control.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be disclosed in the App's release notes.
